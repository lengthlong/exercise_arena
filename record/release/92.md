## Release notes

- The paper detail page (a handed-in daily or check paper) always shows the before/after ladder pair, computed from the paper's own answers.
- When a question with an answer on that paper is selected, a distinct figure (red cap with a gold star, indigo shirt, backpack, question-number tag) hops across to the right ladder and climbs rung by rung to the question's level after the paper; a question that stays hops across and stays, and a question marked wrong slides down. A caption says what happened, for example "Q1 climbs from level 2 to level 3." Check-paper right and partly right answers show "stays", a wrong one shows "drops back to level 1", per the existing ladder rules.
- Motion stops when the ladder is out of view or reduced motion is requested; the figure then rests on its final rung.
