## Release notes

- Recording earns coins: each new question you save gives you 2 coins this week, in the library you have selected. Extra questions added from saved material count too; edits don't. Questions recorded before this version earn nothing. Deleting a question recorded this week takes its 2 coins back.
- The Record page shows your coins this week at the top. A save that earns coins shows a "+N coins" celebration and updates the total.
- Games lists recording coins in "Earned" (for example "3 recorded × 2"). Recording coins also count in balance and in Starboard tie-breaks.
- Record page messages are shorter, one line per outcome:
  - Green means done; amber means something needs checking; red means it failed and nothing was done.
  - A partial save shows what was saved separately from what is left.
  - Old reading messages clear when you change the input.
  - Upload and AI timing moved into a collapsed Diagnostics section.
