## Release notes

- Unfinished daily hand-ins are protected and recoverable: Today lists them even when nothing is due, and a new batch never replaces a paper that already has saved attempts. Missing questions on a recovered paper are stated.
- New daily and check papers start with an explicit empty hand-in marker. Handed in and fully assessed are shown separately.
- Question sets store an owner. New questions can only join a set with the same owner and subject; sets without an owner (older data) cannot be selected for adding questions or edited, and their last member's removal keeps the set. Material and word-bank edits write only what changed and keep every member answer.
- Check-paper creation re-validates its preview against the current library and asks for a fresh preview otherwise.
- A failed required read now says "Couldn't read your data" instead of loading forever; a registration failure warns.
- Kept from the previous production page: database writes that fail with `unavailable` are retried up to twice.
- Not carried over from the previous production page: the separate "resume an interrupted hand-in" behavior (replaced by the protected-paper recovery above), and its rule that a daily paper without a `handedIn` field counts as not handed in (older daily papers with saved attempts are again treated as handed in, per `DATA.md`).
