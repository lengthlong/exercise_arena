## Release notes

- **Papers → PSLE:** a new sub-tab after Exam and Daily for the weekly school-paper plan up to PSLE.
  - A week × subject grid shows Maths, English and Science, with done papers and their scores, papers behind, and this week outlined.
  - Tap a paper to open it on sgexam.com, mark it done with an optional score, undo, or record its mistakes.
  - Until Claude builds a plan, the sub-tab says "No plan yet".
- **Record mistakes:** recording opens with the paper's subject set and Source filled with the paper's name, for example "2023 P6 Prelim Nanyang Maths Paper 2 Q7". Recording itself is unchanged.
- **Settings → Questions per day:** a − / + setting. It drives a weekly bar chart of how many of today's questions are still left, and names the week they would all be mastered.
  - The forecast uses the recent right rate and recent weekly recording.
  - It does not limit Today.
