## Release notes

- The top of the Record page shows a gold hand-written line, "🪙 N coins from recording this week · +2 for each question". It counts only coins earned by recording; daily-redo coins stay on Games.
- While saving, a treasure bowl (聚宝盆) appears in the middle of the screen with coins dancing out of it. Each saved question sends a coin up to the number, which goes up by 2.
- When saving finishes with coins earned:
  - coins and confetti burst from the bowl;
  - a gold "+N coins" badge pops up in its place and flies onto the number, which glows.
- Edits and saves that earn nothing show no animation. With reduced motion turned on, nothing flies or bursts.
