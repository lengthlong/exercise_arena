## Release notes

- One Paper page for every paper. Taking a daily or check paper and reviewing it afterwards are the same page, and a paper re-opened later shows what it showed right after hand-in.
- Re-opened daily papers show their coins and the before and after ladder. Check papers show the ladder with no coins line.
- A written answer without a saved judgment reads "Not judged yet", the outcome says how many are left to judge, and the score keeps them in the total. Written answers still in the current session can be self-judged; a re-opened paper cannot create the missing attempt.
- Any judgment on a paper card can be corrected inline with the Override dropdown, including right after a self-mark. The old lock and the "go to detail" note are gone.
- Every paper row (Today Exam, Papers Exam, Papers Daily) starts an unhanded paper and opens a handed-in one in the same way. Back returns to where you came from, and Detail on a card returns to the same card.
- The page recovers a protected unfinished daily paper if a hand-in fails part-way, keeps its per-question climber on the review ladder, and states missing questions, as in version 92.
