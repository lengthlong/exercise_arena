# sgexam.com P6 prelim papers and PSLE dates

> **Retrieved:** 2026-11-25. **Baseline:** none (public web pages).
> **Relation to earlier notes:** none.
> **Consuming discussion:** 0050.

## 1. Summary

- sgexam.com lists free P6 papers on one page per subject. In 2023–2026 it has **54–55 prelim papers per subject**. A weekly plan from now to PSLE 2027 needs about 43 per subject, so prelims alone cover it.
- Papers are PDF files on Google Drive behind a plain "Download Paper" link. No login was seen. No answer key was seen on the one paper page checked.
- SEAB has not yet published the PSLE 2027 dates. In 2026 the written papers ran from Thu 24 Sep to Wed 30 Sep 2026. Historically they fall in late September or early October.

## 2. sgexam.com structure (fetched 2026-11-25)

- Subject listing pages: `https://sgexam.com/primary-6-maths/`, `https://sgexam.com/primary-6-english/`, `https://sgexam.com/primary-6-science/`. Each is one long page grouped by year from 2026 down to 2011, with no pagination.
- Paper pages (2016 and later): `https://sgexam.com/subject/{subject}/{year}-p6-{subject}-{type}-{school}-pdf/`, for example `2025-p6-maths-prelim-exam-acs-junior-pdf`. The slug follows the link text. Only one slug was checked.
- Homepage banner: "7 Sept 2026 – Updated 2026 Primary 6 Prelim Exam papers (Free)".
- Download: the button links to `drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=…`. The Drive file was not opened.
- Answer keys: none on the one paper page checked (2025 Maths, ACS Junior). Separate "Solution" entries appear only in 2017. English and Science paper pages were not checked.

## 5. PSLE dates

- PSLE 2026 written papers: Thu 24 Sep to Wed 30 Sep 2026, with listening comprehension on 15 Sep 2026 (third-party calendars citing SEAB).
- PSLE 2027: not announced as of 2026-11-25. Check `https://www.seab.gov.sg/home/examinations/psle` and `https://www.moe.gov.sg/national-exams-dates`.
