# Research briefs

Use these when a large subject can be divided among read-only sub-agents. Adapt the wording and keep the requested handoff items.

## Overview brief

```markdown
Research the following external subject for a design workspace. Read only. Return a draft in your final message.

- Topic and main question: <question>
- Subject and entry: <local path or URL>, baseline <commit, version or —>
- Scope: <whole subject or subsection>
- Prior notes for comparison: <paths>
- Read `<workspace>/.agents/skills/design-writing/SKILL.md`.
- Use the relevant parts of the attached note outline. Leave the summary to the lead agent.

Use primary sources and direct observation. Explain conflicts with secondary sources and distinguish current facts, inference and unverified claims.

Return polished applicable sections, promising open questions with reasons for broad research, and primary source locations with commit or retrieval date and limitations.
```

## Follow-up brief

```markdown
Investigate one question about an external subject. Read only and return your draft in your final message.

- Topic and one to five questions: <questions>
- Subject and entry: <local path or URL>, baseline <commit or version>
- Overview: <path, sections to read>
- Read `<workspace>/.agents/skills/design-writing/SKILL.md`.

Return a publishable ## D1. <question> section, original overview sentences needing correction with replacements, and primary source locations and baselines or dates. Distinguish facts, inference and unverified items.
```
